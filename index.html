How to refresh this list from Discogs (CSV export)
  1. Sign in to Discogs, then open https://www.discogs.com/users/export (also under your avatar → Export).
  2. Choose Collection and click Request Data Export. Discogs builds the file in the background — usually a minute or two — and lists it on the same page (it also emails you).
  3. Click Download to get the CSV, then use Import Discogs CSV above. New records are added; existing ones keep their Plex status and notes (matched on release_id).
  4. Your progress lives in this browser's storage for this file. Click Back up progress now and then to save a JSON copy next to this page.
